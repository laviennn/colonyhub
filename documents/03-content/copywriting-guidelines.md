# Copywriting & microcopy guidelines

**Source:** Brand Guidelines pp.8–9. **Creative direction:** Creative × Technology.

## Voice principles

| Do | Don't |
|---|---|
| Precise and plainspoken | Inflated self-praise |
| Warm, professional Indonesian | Stiff bureaucratic sentences |
| Explain technical terms briefly | Jargon for its own sake |
| Show work and trade-offs | Promise outcomes without data |
| Let projects illustrate expertise | Overuse colony/ant metaphors |

## Brand vocabulary

Allowed where meaningful: connected, build, improve, momentum, collaboration, strategy, creative, technology, measured growth, colony, hub. Avoid brand buzzwords such as `solusi inovatif terdepan`, `nomor satu`, `hasil instan`, `terbaik se-Indonesia` (source p.9).

## Tone by journey

- Home: confident, concise, interesting but legible.
- Service: concrete, scope-first and careful about outcomes.
- Work: analytical, honest, specific about constraints and roles.
- Insights: curious, evidence-driven, useful rather than promotional.
- About: human and direct; no invented origin mythology.
- Contact/form: helpful, explicit, non-judgmental.
- Errors: brief, actionable; small amount of brand personality allowed.

## Sample swaps (WORKING COPY)

| Generic | colonyhub treatment |
|---|---|
| Kami adalah agency terbaik untuk solusi digital inovatif | Desain, teknologi, dan strategi dalam satu proses kerja. |
| Transformasikan bisnis Anda ke level berikutnya | Ceritakan tantangan yang sedang Anda hadapi. Kita cari pendekatan yang masuk akal. |
| Portofolio luar biasa kami | Pilihan pekerjaan, berikut konteks dan keputusan di baliknya. |
| Solusi terdepan dengan hasil spektakuler | Apa yang kami bangun, mengapa, dan bagaimana hasilnya dinilai. |

## Headline criteria

- Distinct message per page; ideally readable without the subtitle.
- Prefer strong verbs and useful nouns over wordplay alone.
- Do not combine 3 or more vague motivational taglines.
- If English headline is used, Indonesian description should clearly explain its meaning for local audience.
- Title case is not mandatory; use sentence case/lowercase per brand guidance.

## CTA lexicon

Primary: `Diskusikan proyek`, `Mulai percakapan`, `Start a Project`.  
Secondary: `Lihat pekerjaan`, `Jelajahi layanan`, `Baca artikel`, `Lihat proses`.  
Avoid manipulative urgency, fake scarcity and generic repeated `Learn more` without accessible context.

## Form microcopy

- Label `Email kerja` rather than only placeholder.
- `Ceritakan proyek atau tantangan Anda` with explanation of useful details.
- `Belum yakin layanan apa yang dibutuhkan? Tidak masalah.` for optional choices.
- Show input-level error: `Masukkan alamat email yang valid.`
- Success only after backend confirmation; don't promise response time until verified.

## Editorial writing rules

- Open with the problem/question, not `Di era digital saat ini...`.
- Include concrete details, assumptions, limits, examples or evidence.
- Define terms at first use if audience requires.
- Use short paragraphs, descriptive H2s, meaningful image captions.
- No manufactured statistics, testimonials or client quotes.
- Label hypothetical examples explicitly.
- Preserve factual authorship and citation attribution.

## Localization

**OPEN:** whether to launch Indonesia only or bilingual. Proposed first release: Indonesian body and accessible navigation, with selectively English display phrases only when clarity survives. For bilingual launch, write each locale natively, never machine-translate unreviewed, set language metadata and hreflang correctly.

## Review checklist

- Does every claim have evidence or a clear qualifier?
- Could the same text appear on any agency website? If yes, add specificity.
- Does the user know what the next step is?
- Are internal metaphors used sparingly?
- Are all dates, figures, services and promises approved?
