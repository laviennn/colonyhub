# Positioning, audiences & messaging hypotheses

**Source:** Brand Guidelines pp.1–3, with research and website planning proposals identified below.

## Source positioning

The brand serves SMEs, growth startups, B2B/corporate customers and connects strategy, creative, technology and performance. Its positioning statement promises measurable growth without the coordination overhead of multiple vendors.

## Recommended website proposition (PROPOSED)

> Strategy, design and technology. One connected team.

Indonesia-first body copy:

> Kami menghubungkan strategi, desain, pengembangan web, dan pertumbuhan digital dalam satu proses kerja yang jelas.

The promise should be concrete: one accountable point of contact, clear scope, deliverable visibility, regular progress communication, and measurable work where possible. Never imply verified client performance until proof is supplied.

## Target buyer journeys (HYPOTHESES TO VALIDATE)

| Segment | Trigger | Main concern | Proof sought | Best entry point |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| SME owner/marketing lead | Need brand/site refresh | Cost, clarity, vendor coordination | Related projects and scope | Services → Contact |
| Startup founder/product lead | Need launch or iteration | Speed, engineering reliability | Build case studies, process | Work → Approach |
| B2B marketing leader | Need qualified demand | Reliability, measurable impact | SEO/content and metrics | Insights → Services |

These are assumptions for discovery interviews, not confirmed behavioral research.

## Jobs to be done (HYPOTHESES)

1. When I need a website that reflects my business, I want an agency that understands both design and implementation so I can launch confidently.
2. When digital efforts are split across vendors, I need shared accountability and communication so fewer things fall between teams.
3. When investing in growth activities, I need visibility into work and outcomes so decisions are not based on vague claims.

## Messaging hierarchy

1. **Clarity:** explain what we actually do.
2. **Proof:** show relevant work, processes and decisions.
3. **Approach:** demonstrate connected disciplines and accountability.
4. **Conversion:** make consultation easy without an overlong questionnaire.
5. **Depth:** publish useful insights and field notes.

## Message by context

| Context | Core idea | Evidence module |
|---|---|---|
| Homepage | Independent thinking, connected execution | Selected work |
| Services | A specialist team for each part of the problem | Deliverables + related projects |
| Work | Design decisions have real constraints | Before/after, process, outcome |
| Approach | The relationship is structured and transparent | Stages, review checkpoints |
| Insights | We share what we learn | Firsthand research and practical examples |
| Contact | A useful first conversation | Brief requirements, response expectation only if confirmed |

## What not to say

Avoid: “#1 agency”, “100% guaranteed ranking”, “infinite growth”, “AI-powered everything”, “world-class team” without support, “we help every business succeed.” Avoid fabricated social proof.

## Decisions before marketing copy is locked

- **OPEN:** priority client segment for first six months.
- **OPEN:** top 1–2 lead service lines to feature in hero and paid campaigns.
- **OPEN:** approved work evidence and permission to publish client identifiers.
- **OPEN:** verified contact channels and response policy.
- **OPEN:** markets/language beyond Indonesia.
