# Brand foundation — colonyhub

**Status:** Brand facts extracted from source PDF; website interpretations clearly labeled.  
**Source:** *Brand Identity Guidelines — colonyhub*, v1.0, pp. 1–13.

## 1. Identity (SOURCE)

- Brand name: **colonyhub**, always lowercase and single word except formal legal use (p.1).
- Business type: integrated digital agency.
- Metaphor: **colony** = collaborative specialists, collective strength; **hub** = connection, coordination and central point of solutions.
- Core statement: connected digital team moving together to accelerate business growth (p.1).
- Purpose: make digital growth structured, measurable, attainable for businesses of different sizes.
- Vision: become a digital hub of choice for Indonesia and Southeast Asia (p.1).
- Tagline: **Grow together. Move as one.** Local version: **Tumbuh bareng, gerak serempak.** (p.3).

## 2. Values and observed behaviors (SOURCE, p.2)

| Value | Operating behavior |
|---|---|
| Together | One cross-functional client team instead of silos |
| Precision | Data-informed decisions and rigorous QA |
| Momentum | Weekly sprints and regular reporting |
| Craft | Consistent design and code standards |
| Openness | Transparent dashboards and communication |

Brand personality: smart, warm, agile, confident without arrogance, collaborative and modern.

## 3. Target clients (SOURCE, p.2)

1. Indonesian SMEs/local brands seeking quality branding, websites and marketing.
2. Growth-stage startups needing fast external teams and iteration.
3. B2B companies/corporates needing credibility, SEO, content engine and lead generation.

**Important:** The document lists all three but does not rank them. Prioritizing one is a **proposal**, not an existing brand decision.

## 4. Promise and differentiation (SOURCE, pp.2–3)

Integrated strategy, creative, technology and performance under one roof. Claimed differentiators: one hub/all disciplines, growth-first, transparent reporting, and SEO + GEO readiness.

**OPEN:** establish which of these differentiators is verifiable and credible through real case studies. Do not publish guarantees or invented numbers.

## 5. Service architecture (SOURCE, pp.3–4)

- **Strategy Unit:** brand strategy, research, positioning, roadmap.
- **Creative Unit:** identity, graphics, motion, content and video.
- **Build Unit:** websites, landing pages, e-commerce, web apps, automation.
- **Growth Unit:** SEO, GEO, content, performance ads.
- **Social Unit:** social, community, KOL/influencer.
- **Data Unit:** analytics, dashboard, CRM and reporting.

Packages: **Seed** (digital foundations), **Grow** (monthly growth retainer), **Scale** (dedicated integrated team). These are conceptual packages, *not* defined priced offers.

## 6. Logo rules (SOURCE, pp.4–5)

Concept: central circle plus 5–6 small connected circles in a subtle open `C`, or hex-based alternative. Wordmark lowercase geometric/rounded; `colony` semibold and `hub` in an accent color. Suggested variants: horizontal, stacked, symbol-only, wordmark-only, monochrome. Minimum sizes: symbol 16px / 8mm; horizontal 96px / 25mm. Clear space minimum equals central circle height. Do not stretch, rotate, shadow, recolor outside palette, or place over busy photography without overlay.

**OPEN:** final symbol, logo vector, exact master lockups and wordmark font. Design files do not exist in this document. Do not treat a typographic placeholder `colonyhub.` as final logo.

## 7. Color (SOURCE, pp.6–7)

| Token | Hex | Primary role |
|---|---|---|
| Hub Navy | `#0B1230` | Dark base, prominent text |
| Colony Periwinkle | `#8A94F2` | Brand, highlights |
| Signal Lime | `#C6F432` | CTA, small energetic accents |
| Cloud White | `#F6F4EE` | Light surfaces |
| Slate | `#5A6178` | Secondary text |
| Mist | `#E4E6F0` | Dividers and section surfaces |
| Coral Pulse | `#FF6B4A` | Limited secondary alert/illustration |
| Deep Indigo | `#3F4BB8` | Links/hover on light, gradient |

Suggested composition: **60% base navy/white, 25% periwinkle, 10% lime, 5% accent**. Gradient **Colony Glow** periwinkle to deep indigo, 135°; lime remains solid.

Accessibility guidance in source: white on navy/deep indigo; navy text on periwinkle and lime; avoid white on lime, lime text on white.

## 8. Typography (SOURCE, p.7)

- Display: **Sora** 600–700; alternative **Space Grotesk**.
- Body/UI: **Inter** 400–500.
- Technical labels/data: **JetBrains Mono** 400–500.
- Desktop scale: H1 56/64px (1.1), H2 40px (1.2), H3 28px (1.3), H4 20px (1.4), body 16–18px (1.6), caption 13px (1.5).
- Prefer sentence case or lowercase headings; avoid long ALL CAPS.

## 9. Voice (SOURCE, pp.8–9)

Clear, warm, to-the-point, grounded, conversational-professional Indonesian. Avoid jargon, boasting, hyperbole and unrealistic promises. Semantic fields: colony, hub, connected, momentum, sprint, growing together. Do not force metaphors into every paragraph.

## 10. Visual grammar (SOURCE, pp.9–10)

- Node/link pattern and secondary hex grid; rounded cards 12–20px; 1.5px connecting lines.
- Outline icon 1.5–2px with rounded corners and restrained accent.
- Documentary collaboration imagery, not staged handshake photography.
- Purposeful 200–400ms ease-out motion.
- Twelve-column grid, generous whitespace, one key message per screen and strong CTA.
- Navy hero; Cloud White content; case studies with metrics, process and testimonials where real.

## 11. Chosen website direction (USER DECISION)

**Creative × Technology**: art direction and visual craft of a creative studio combined with credible engineering, strategy, analysis and execution. Emphasize selected work, case studies and writing; avoid looking like an interchangeable SaaS template.

## 12. Proposed translation (RECOMMENDATION)

- Editorial typography-first landing, not 3D spectacle.
- Show work early; demonstrate engineering through project details and process choices.
- Clear client outcomes without manufactured metrics.
- Distinctive nodes/connections as *small, functional visual signatures*.
- Use campaign-like pacing with sober UI discipline and performance budgets.
