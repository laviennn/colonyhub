# Implementation roadmap & milestones

**Status:** Proposed delivery plan; no actual project dates or staffing commitments.  
**Source alignment:** brand guide proposes 90-day brand launch (pp.12–13), but this is a website workstream plan that must be integrated with brand operations.

## Phase 0 — Decisions and inventory

**Outputs:** approved brand-source interpretation, chosen audience/lead service, final logo plan, content audit, case-study permissions, technical feasibility, privacy ownership.  
**Exit:** no blocking claims or asset gaps for wireframes.

## Phase 1 — Product and content architecture

**Outputs:** PRD sign-off, sitemap, page blueprint, user journeys, CMS schema draft, SEO route map, analytics event definitions.  
**Exit:** page purposes and minimum content are agreed.

## Phase 2 — Design system & wireframes

**Outputs:** low-fi flows, hero explorations, responsive typography and grid, component inventory, key state design, sample actual content composition.  
**Exit:** UX routes validated by internal walkthrough; accessibility and contrast checks begun.

## Phase 3 — High-fidelity creative system

**Outputs:** final logo integration, Figma component library (if used), desktop/tablet/mobile screens, motion reference, export rules, editorial and case-study templates.  
**Exit:** representative pages approved with real content, not lorem ipsum.

## Phase 4 — Implementation foundation

**Outputs:** selected framework/CMS/hosting, repositories, CI, routing, tokens, global UI components, content adapters, preview/staging and SEO utilities.  
**Exit:** editors can preview and developers can deploy safely.

## Phase 5 — Page and CMS build

**Outputs:** Home, Work, case studies, Services, details, Approach, Insights listing/articles, About, Contact, legal utility pages and system states.  
**Exit:** all P0 feature acceptance criteria checked.

## Phase 6 — Content, SEO and QA

**Outputs:** approved real projects, source-checked articles, final alt/metadata, sitemap/schema, contact delivery, responsive/browser/a11y/performance QA.  
**Exit:** release checklist signed off; no known critical blockers.

## Phase 7 — Launch and measurement

**Outputs:** production deploy, analytics validation, monitoring, redirects, Search Console setup, bug triage and documented content operating rhythm.  
**Exit:** inquiries arrive reliably and editorial owners can publish independently.

## Workstreams and responsibilities

| Workstream | Suggested owner | Handoff |
|---|---|---|
| Brand & positioning | Brand/product lead | Approved claims/assets |
| UX / visual | Designer | Responsive screens and component specs |
| Content | Editor / subject experts | Verified copy, work, articles |
| Engineering | Tech lead | CMS, public routes, forms, monitoring |
| SEO & analytics | Growth specialist | Metadata, indexation and event tracking |
| Legal/privacy | Authorized owner | Data-policy approval |

Owners are roles, not assigned individuals.

## MVP launch gates

- Core pages live with real content.
- At least one genuinely publishable case study OR transparent internal proof and no misleading framing.
- Insights index and real articles (quantity chosen by editorial readiness).
- End-to-end contact form tested.
- Logo and legal details approved.
- Accessibility, SEO and performance checked.
- Editors trained and rollback process documented.

## Future enhancements (only after evidence)

- Search across articles, advanced Work filters, multilingual publishing, service landing pages for campaigns, richer metrics component, public careers, newsletter with consent and CRM integration.

## Risks to schedule

Missing work assets, unpublished projects, delayed brand logo decision, no CMS editor, unclear legal owner, team overloading 6 services. Resolve content and owner dependencies before high-fidelity polish.
